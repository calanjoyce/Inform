# Keithwood

An Inform 7 game in progress, set in the house I grew up in, layering memories of the past over the present. The design notes are in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Front yard demo

A small playable sketch for trying out the time shift: the front yard and the gravel strip along the road. Things that are gone have left traces: tire tracks on the gravel strip and the driveway, a slight mound in the lawn where the old tree stood, and a post hole where the mailbox used to be. Look closely at a trace, touch it or search it, and what made it comes back. The cars and the tree are hazy, so each time you look they may shift to another of the ways they might have been. Left alone, they fade.

## Files

- `story.ni`: the Inform 7 source, with comments explaining each part.
- `Keithwood.gblorb`: the compiled demo (Glulx, release build), made with Inform 7 v10.1.2.
- `walkthrough.txt`: how the demo works, with a sample playthrough.
- `DESIGN.md`: the design notes.

## Playing it

Open `Keithwood.gblorb` in any Glulx interpreter: Lectrote or Gargoyle on the desktop, or upload it at [iplayif.com](https://iplayif.com/). Type `hint` for help.

To work on the source, paste `story.ni` into a new Inform project and press **Go**. Type `test me` at the first prompt to run through the demo automatically (testing builds only).
